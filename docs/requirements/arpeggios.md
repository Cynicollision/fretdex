# Arpeggios (`ARP`)

**Retired 2026-11-25 (owner).** Every requirement here is `Retired`, as the module isn't worth its place: an arpeggio is its scale with only the chord's notes, which a player reads off the scale card by highlighting them (SCL-2.3). The chord card shows a chord's tones on the neck instead, over the current key's scale (CHD-2.5). Kept for reference; the IDs aren't reused.

Chord tones laid out like a scale: the arpeggios module reuses the scale card's fretboard and position system with only the chord's notes.

Screens: **Arpeggios** (`/arpeggios`) and the arpeggio card (`/arpeggios/[root]/[quality]`, option `pos`).

## ARP-1 The user can see a chord's tones across the neck

| ID | Requirement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| ARP-1.1 | The list shall offer a root picker and the triad and seventh qualities of the chords list (CHD-1.1), about 13; extended chords are better read off a scale. | Retired |
| ARP-1.2 | The card shall show the arpeggio's notes and intervals, every chord tone across the fret range, and a small keyboard. | Retired |
| ARP-1.3 | On guitar, the card shall offer five positions following the chord's CAGED shapes in standard tuning, the choice being `pos`. | Retired |
| ARP-1.4 | On bass, the card shall offer one- and two-octave shapes from the root on each of the lowest two strings. | Retired |
| ARP-1.5 | The card shall link to the chord card for the same root and quality (CHD), and offer to overlay the arpeggio on its parent scale in the current key (scale notes dimmed). | Retired |
| ARP-1.6 | Pinned arpeggios shall be listed first (PIN-3). | Retired |
| ARP-1.7 | In tunings other than standard, the card shall show the full neck only, without CAGED positions. | Retired |
